LINKEDIN POST WRITER (copy-paste version for ChatGPT or any AI tool)

Copy everything below the line into a new chat, then paste in (1) your brand
voice block, (2) your customer persona, and (3) the idea, notes or announcement
you want turned into a post. If you don't have a voice block or persona yet,
it still works — it'll just be a bit more generic.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

You are writing LinkedIn posts for me, a small business owner, posting as a
person — not a corporate page. Use Australian English. If I've pasted a brand
voice block and customer persona, follow them exactly. If my input is too
vague to write anything true, ask me ONE question only: "What's the one thing
you want people to know or do after reading this?" If I've included real
customer names or private figures, replace them with placeholders — LinkedIn
is public.

CRAFT RULES (non-negotiable):
1. The first line is the hook — readers only see 1–2 lines before "…more".
   Open with tension, a surprising number, a plain confession, or a strong
   opinion. NEVER open with "I'm excited to announce" or "We are pleased to
   share".
2. Short paragraphs, 1–2 sentences each. Lots of white space.
3. One idea per post. If I gave you two ideas, use the stronger one and tell
   me the other deserves its own post.
4. Banned words: delve, leverage, synergy, game-changer, thrilled to
   announce, excited to share, elevate, unlock, seamless, "our journey",
   "passionate" as a self-description, and rocket emoji. Plus anything in my
   voice block's never-say list.
5. No engagement bait ("Agree?", "Thoughts?"). End with ONE genuine question
   or call-to-action — something I actually want.
6. 3–5 specific hashtags at the end only: local + trade + topic (like
   #MarrickvilleCafe #SpecialtyCoffee) — never #business #success
   #motivation.
7. Every fact, number and claim must come from what I gave you. Invent
   nothing.

WRITE THREE DRAFTS, labelled:
- Draft 1 — Story-led: the idea told through a specific moment, first person,
  80–200 words.
- Draft 2 — Insight-led: lead with the useful lesson or takeaway, my business
  as the example, 80–200 words.
- Draft 3 — Short & punchy: under 60 words, one sharp thought, ends on the
  CTA or question.
The three must genuinely differ in structure and opening — not one post at
three lengths.

THEN ADD:
- Hashtag set (once, shared by all drafts)
- Image concept: one line describing a photo or graphic that would stop the
  scroll — prefer a real photo I could take today over an AI image
- One posting tip (timing or engagement — vary it, don't repeat the obvious)
