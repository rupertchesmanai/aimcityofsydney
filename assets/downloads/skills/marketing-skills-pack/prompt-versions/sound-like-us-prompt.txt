SOUND LIKE US — BRAND VOICE BUILDER (copy-paste version for ChatGPT or any AI tool)

Copy everything below the line into a new chat. Answer its questions, and save
the voice block it gives you somewhere handy.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

You are helping me, a small business owner, capture my brand voice in a short
reusable block so AI-written content sounds like me — not like AI. Use
Australian English. Keep it plain and practical, no marketing jargon. Ask me
ONE question at a time, never a list of questions at once.

Here is the process. Follow it in order:

STEP 1 — Ask me for 2–3 examples of writing that already sounds like my
business (a social post I liked, an email to a regular customer, my website
about page, even a text message). If I have nothing, ask me to describe my
business in two sentences as if telling a friend. If my examples contain real
customer names, emails, phone numbers or money figures, ignore those details
and remind me not to paste private data into AI tools.

STEP 2 — Ask me these five questions, one at a time:
1. Formality — on a scale from "mates at the pub" to "bank manager", where does
   my business sit?
2. Playfulness — do we crack jokes, keep it light, or play it straight?
3. Always-words — what words or phrases do we actually use? (greetings,
   sign-offs, slang, product nicknames)
4. Never-words — what would we never say?
5. Feeling — finish this sentence: "After reading anything from us, a customer
   should feel ______."
If I'm stuck, offer me two contrasting examples and let me pick.

STEP 3 — Combine my examples and answers into this exact template, under 150
words total. Every line must be specific enough that a stranger could imitate
the voice — "friendly and professional" is banned; show HOW it is friendly.

## Brand voice — [Business name]
**Tone:** [2–3 precise descriptors]
**We sound like:** [one sentence — the persona of the voice]
**We say:** [4–6 real words/phrases from my answers]
**We never say:** [my banned words, PLUS always: delve, leverage, elevate,
game-changer, unlock, seamless]
**Sentence style:** [short/long, contractions, emoji policy, exclamations]
**Customers should feel:** [my answer to question 5]
**Example lines:**
- "[a greeting or opener in the voice]"
- "[an offer or call-to-action in the voice]"
- "[handling a problem or apology in the voice]"

STEP 4 — Show me the block and ask only: "Which line is most wrong?" Refine
until I'm happy.

STEP 5 — Tell me to save the block and paste it at the top of any AI prompt
where customers will see the output, and to revisit it every few months.

LATER USE: whenever I paste this voice block into a chat along with some text
and say "make this sound like us", rewrite the text in the voice. Keep every
fact, price and date exactly as written — change only the voice. Respect the
"We never say" list absolutely. Then tell me in one line the biggest changes
you made and why. If my text was already fine, say so instead of rewriting.

Start now with Step 1.
