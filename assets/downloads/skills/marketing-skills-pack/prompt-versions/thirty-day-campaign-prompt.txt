30-DAY CAMPAIGN BUILDER (copy-paste version for ChatGPT or any AI tool)

Copy everything below the line into a new chat, then paste in (1) your brand
voice block, (2) your customer persona, and (3) the product, service or
promotion you want to campaign around. It works without the voice block and
persona, just more generically.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

You are turning ONE product, service or promotion from my small business into
a four-week campaign I can actually execute while running the business. Use
Australian English. Every item must be doable in under an hour by one busy
person.

THE FIXED ARC (never change the order):
- Week 1 — AWARENESS: introduce the problem, share insights, build curiosity.
  No selling.
- Week 2 — EDUCATION: how-to content, tips, behind the scenes. Be useful.
- Week 3 — TRUST: testimonials, results, proof. Let others do the talking.
- Week 4 — OFFER: CTA, urgency, make it easy to buy or book. Now sell.

FIRST, ask me one at a time (skip anything I've already told you):
1. What problem does my offer solve — in the customer's words?
2. Who is it for? (If I gave you a persona, confirm instead of re-asking.)
3. What's the offer and CTA — what they get, what it costs, how they buy or
   book, and any REAL deadline or limit?
Then in ONE combined question: which channels I actually use, and what proof
I have for Week 3 (reviews, before/afters, numbers, happy customers). If I
have no proof, Week 3 becomes "collect and show proof" — asking for reviews,
documenting jobs — NEVER invented testimonials.

THEN BUILD THE CALENDAR — 3 posts per week, 12 total (cap at 5/week if I ask
for more, and tell me consistency beats volume). For each item give me a
table row: Day | Channel | Format | Working title | One-line brief.

Row rules:
- Spread days across the week; only use channels I actually have.
- Vary formats: post, story, reel/video, email, Google Business update.
- Working titles specific enough to write from ("The 3 questions we ask
  before quoting any repaint"), never "educational post".
- Briefs say what it shows and its micro-CTA. Weeks 1–3 micro-CTAs are
  engagement only (follow, save, reply); ONLY Week 4 asks for the sale.
- Pace Week 4 around any real deadline: announce → remind → last call. Never
  invent urgency I didn't give you.

FINISH WITH:
1. A campaign header: the offer, who it's for, the CTA, one line per week
   theme.
2. "First three things this week" — exactly three concrete actions from the
   actual plan (like texting my two happiest customers for Google reviews
   before Week 3 needs them).

Never invent testimonials, results, statistics, deadlines or discounts.

Here's my offer:
[PASTE YOUR PRODUCT / SERVICE / PROMOTION HERE]
