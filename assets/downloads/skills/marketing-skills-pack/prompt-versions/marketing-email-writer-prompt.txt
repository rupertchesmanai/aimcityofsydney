MARKETING EMAIL WRITER (copy-paste version for ChatGPT or any AI tool)

Copy everything below the line into a new chat, then paste in (1) your brand
voice block, (2) your customer persona, and (3) what the email is about. It
still works without the voice block and persona, just more generically.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

You are writing a customer-facing marketing email for my small business. Use
Australian English. If I've pasted a brand voice block and customer persona,
follow them exactly. Write to ONE person ("you"), never "our valued
customers". Use [First name] as a merge-field placeholder — never a real
customer name.

FIRST, work out which type of email this is (ask me one question if unclear):
- Offer/promotion — announce a deal, product or event; the offer is the star
- Follow-up/check-in — after a purchase or visit; warm, brief, zero selling
- Rebooking nudge — time for their next appointment; helpful, easy yes
- Thank-you — sincere, generous, smallest possible ask or none
- Newsletter — chatty roundup; value first, promotion last
- Win-back — lapsed customers; honest and light, with a reason to return

If I haven't given you the concrete details (what/when/how much, who it's
going to, and the ONE thing the reader should do), ask for them in a single
combined question. NEVER invent an offer, discount amount or deadline.

CRAFT RULES (non-negotiable):
1. 3 subject line options, each under 50 characters: one straight, one
   curious, one personal or urgent. Subject and body must tell the same
   truth. No ALL CAPS; one emoji max and only if my voice allows.
2. One preview-text line (~40–90 characters) that extends the subject rather
   than repeating it.
3. ONE goal, ONE call-to-action — stated once mid-email, at most once more at
   the end, same wording both times ("Book your spot", "Claim the offer").
4. Mobile-first: 60–150 words (newsletters up to 250), paragraphs of 1–3
   short sentences, point clear from the first two lines.
5. Open with MY READER's situation, not my news ("Your next service is about
   due" beats "It's been six months since…!").
6. Concrete beats clever: dates, prices, how-to-redeem in plain sight. Real
   scarcity only — never manufactured urgency.
7. Sign off with my actual name, not "The Team".
8. Banned words: delve, leverage, elevate, game-changer, unlock, seamless,
   "valued customer", "don't miss out", plus my voice block's never-say
   list.

DELIVER IN THIS ORDER:
1. The 3 subject options (labelled)
2. Preview text
3. The email (greeting with [First name] → body → CTA → sign-off)
4. A plain-text variant (short lines, CTA as a bare link line) for sending
   without an email platform
5. One-line reminder: Australian marketing emails must identify the
   business, go only to people who've agreed to hear from me, and honour
   unsubscribes — keep the platform's unsubscribe footer.
