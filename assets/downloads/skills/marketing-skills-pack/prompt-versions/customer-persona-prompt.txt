You are helping me, a small business owner, build a customer persona: one
short, concrete paragraph describing my ideal customer that I can paste into
any marketing prompt so AI writes for the right person. Use Australian
English. Keep it plain — no marketing jargon like "avatar", "segment" or
"ICP". Ask me ONE question at a time, never a list.

Ask me these four questions in order:

QUESTION 1 — Who are they? Tell me to think of my favourite actual customer —
the one I wish I had fifty of — and describe them. Give them a first name and
a one-line description. Age, life situation or job only if it matters to
buying.

QUESTION 2 — What problems do they have that I solve? Get the problems in the
CUSTOMER'S own words, not business-speak (not "lack of colour consultation"
but "the living room's looked tired for two years"). Get 2–3, and ask which
one actually makes the phone ring.

QUESTION 3 — Where do they spend time online? Which platforms and HOW —
scrolling Instagram at night, local Facebook groups, googling "near me",
reading Google reviews before making contact.

QUESTION 4 — What would make them buy or book today? The trigger: a moment, an
offer, a reassurance, or proof. Get the single strongest one.

If my answers reveal genuinely different customer types, offer to build up to
three personas, each labelled. Don't force it — one is right for most
businesses.

Then compress my answers into a single paragraph, UNDER 100 WORDS, shaped like
this:

## Customer persona — [Name] ([Business name])
[Name] is [one-line description]. [Their biggest problem in their words, plus
one or two secondary problems]. They spend time [where + how, online].
They'll buy/book when [the trigger], and what wins them over is [the
reassurance or proof that closes it].

Rules: concrete over general; only details I gave you — never invent
statistics or demographics; plain prose that reads as natural instruction. If
I gave you any real customer names or details, do NOT use them — the persona
is a composite with a made-up name.

Show me the paragraph and ask only: "Does this sound like a real customer
you've actually served?" If something rings false, fix that detail rather than
rewriting everything.

Finish by telling me to paste this paragraph at the top of any marketing
prompt (posts, emails, campaigns, ads), that it pairs with my brand voice
block (persona = who I'm talking to, voice = how I sound), and to update it
when my business changes.
