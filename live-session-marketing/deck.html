Part 1 · Back to basics

Every good marketing prompt has three layers

1 · Your persona

Who you’re talking to — one saved paragraph, reused everywhere

+

2 · Your voice

How you sound — your saved brand-voice block

+

3 · The ask

Act · Explain · Please — the one formula from the workshop

That’s it. No advanced tricks. When a post sounds generic, one of these three layers is missing.

Part 1 · Built live, slowly

One simple, effective post

Act as my social media writer. Explain: here is my customer persona [paste persona] and my brand voice block [paste voice block]. We’re a [your business] and this week we want to tell people about [one thing]. Please write one short Instagram post — a hook first line, 2–3 sentences, one clear call to action, no hashtags yet.
NewThis is the whole game. One post, done well, beats five rushed ones. We’ll do it twice so you see every step.

Part 3 · Built in front of you

A semi-automated month, end to end

  • Monthly schedule: first Monday — AI drafts your 30-day plan using your persona + voice
  • Weekly schedule: Monday morning — it turns this week’s idea into five channel-ready drafts
  • You stay in the loop: nothing publishes itself — you review, tweak and post
  • Start tiny: one scheduled task, one routine. Not your whole marketing at once.

real becomes the differentiator

AI helps you tell your real story more often — it should never replace it.
